Pharmacy Layout Redesign

Siemens Jack · RULA/REBA · Arena · DMAIC · EOQ

Oct 2022 - Apr 2023 · 4-person team · ergonomics & human factors lead

  • A working pharmacy in Dearborn - CornerMed - as the client, with its owner as primary contact
  • My part: Jack posture studies from 5th-percentile female to 95th-percentile male - RULA lowered at every station
  • Team Arena study: 8 layout and staffing variants, 2-sample t-tests - at most 4 seconds to gain, so keep the layout
  • Ordering change instead: 56.3% less safety-stock holding cost
