Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in "hosting setup"?

I'll set up your website on a reliable hosting platform (Netlify, Vercel, or similar) and handle all the technical configuration. The first year of hosting is included in your package price.

How long does it take to build my website?

Timeline depends on your plan: Basic (1 week), Starter (1-2 weeks), Professional (2-3 weeks), Premium (3-4 weeks). This includes design, development, testing, and launch.

What happens after the first year of hosting?

After the first year, you can continue with the same hosting provider (usually $5-10/month) or I can help you transition to a different provider.

Do you provide content writing?

Yes! Starter plans include basic content writing, while Professional and Premium plans include professional content writing for all pages. This includes SEO-optimized copy that converts visitors into customers.

What if I need changes after launch?

All plans include a support period after launch. For ongoing changes, I recommend one of our maintenance plans.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade to a higher plan at any time. The upgrade cost will be the difference between your current plan and the new plan.
